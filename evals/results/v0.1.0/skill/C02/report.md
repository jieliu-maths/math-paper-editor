No definite error found within the checked scope. Read all of `paper/main.tex` and checked both the proposition and final estimate. The main mechanism is compactness of the subcritical Sobolev embedding on the bounded smooth unit ball. The stated range 1≤q<2* avoids the critical endpoint.

At `prop:critical` (lines 14–21), weak H_0^1 convergence gives boundedness. Compact embedding gives strongly L^q-convergent subsubsequences from every subsequence. Their limits equal u because the continuous embedding carries weak H_0^1 convergence to weak L^q convergence (or by distributional uniqueness for q=1 as well). If full strong convergence failed, a subsequence staying a fixed positive distance from u would contradict this compactness argument. The short proof is valid; one sentence spelling out the subsequence argument would improve accessibility without changing its claim.

The square-difference estimate (lines 24–30) follows from factorization, Cauchy–Schwarz, and boundedness of the strongly convergent L² sequence. The displayed limit is justified.
