\begin{lemma}\label{lem:nonnegative}
Let $f$ be a continuous real-valued function on $[0,1]$. Then
$\int_0^1 |f(x)|^2\,dx\ge0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The integrand is nonnegative at every point of $[0,1]$.
\end{proof}
