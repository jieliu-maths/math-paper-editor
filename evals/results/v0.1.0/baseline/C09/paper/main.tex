\begin{lemma}\label{lem:hilbert}
Every bounded sequence in $\ell^2(\mathbb N)$ has a strongly convergent subsequence.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Weak sequential compactness gives a strongly convergent subsequence.
This proves the assertion.
\end{proof}
