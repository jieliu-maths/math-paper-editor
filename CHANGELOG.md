# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-30

First public release, developed from the author's earlier local skill.

- Four scoped modes: review, edit, deep-review, and rewrite.
- Compact English entrypoint with conditional review, analysis/PDE, rewrite,
  and editing references; English and Chinese public documentation.
- Schema-2 snapshots, guarded replacement, separate editor/observed/unattributed
  diffs, integrity checks, and sealed final records.
- Previewable versioned installation, exact-baseline legacy adoption,
  modification-preserving upgrades, and full old-installation backups.
- Ten synthetic behavior cases, isolated baseline/skill preparation, an initial
  model evaluation record, deterministic tests, and macOS/Linux CI.
- MIT license and contribution/release guidance.

Pre-public schema-1 records are not rewritten. Keep their original helper from
the retained installation backup to finish those runs. Model-assisted review
does not certify mathematical correctness.
